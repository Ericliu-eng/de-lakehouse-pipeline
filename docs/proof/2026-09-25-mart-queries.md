## Observations

- The volume ranking and the latest-price snapshot cover all 10 symbols through
  2026-09-25. Those latest days come from Tiingo, because the saved Alpha
  Vantage payloads end on 2026-09-18.
- In Question 3, `close_range` is 0 on every row. The warehouse holds one bar per
  symbol and trading day, so `mart_daily_symbol_summary` aggregates a single
  close: its average, minimum, and maximum are identical. The mart only adds
  information for intraday data; a daily high-low range or a weekly or monthly
  rollup would be more useful at this grain.
